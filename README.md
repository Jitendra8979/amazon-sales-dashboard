# 🛒 Amazon Sales & Delivery Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
Is project mein raw e-commerce transaction data ko ek interactive Excel dashboard mein transform kiya gaya hai. Iska main objective sales performance, regional profitability, aur delivery fulfillment metrics ko analyze karna hai. Advanced Excel functions aur dynamic KPI formatting ka use karke, yeh dashboard business health ka clear view provide karta hai.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data Cleaning, Data Modeling, aur Dashboarding
* **Functions & Features Used:** `XLOOKUP`, `IFERROR`, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Data Validation, Slicers, aur Conditional Formatting

## 🔍 Methodology
1. **Data Structuring:** Product masters, customer masters, aur regional goals ko ek unified relational data model mein consolidate kiya gaya.
2. **Metrics Calculation:** Total Cost, Profit Margins, aur Delivery Lead Times (Days) nikalne ke liye calculated fields banaye gaye.
3. **Status Tracking:** Orders ko 'Fast', 'Slow', 'In-Transit', 'Delivered', ya 'Cancelled' mein categorize karke delivery performance tracker banaya gaya.
4. **Dashboard Design:** Month, Customer Type, aur Region ke slicers ka use karke ek interactive executive reporting interface design kiya gaya.

## 📈 Key Business Insights
Latest 100 transaction records (Total Sales Volume: **$35,258**) ke analysis se yeh key insights mili hain:

* **The Cancellation Bottleneck:** Jabki 34% orders successfully deliver hue hain (**$13,656** realized revenue) aur 36% in-transit hain, business ka cancellation rate **30%** hai jo ki ek major concern hai. *Recommendation: Logistics aur fulfillment partners (DHL, UPS, Amazon Logistics) ke performance ko investigate kiya jaye.*
* **Category Dominance:** **Electronics** sabse best-performing category hai, jiski total sales sabse zyada hai (**$15,209**) aur finalized delivered sales bhi highest hai (**$6,126**). Iske baad Home goods ka number aata hai.
* **Regional Profitability:** **North** region sabse profitable market hai, jisme **$748** net profit generate hua hai (East region ke $222 ke comparison mein bahut zyada).
* **Customer Value:** *Prime* members ne sabse zyada initial checkout volume ($12,387) generate kiya, lekin **Business (B2B)** customers ke *successfully delivered* sales sabse highest hain ($6,793), jo inhe sabse reliable aur valuable customer segment banata hai.
